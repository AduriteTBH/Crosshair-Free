## 💡 Why Crosshair Free?

Most commercial crosshair apps (like Crosshair X) charge money, require Steam/subscriptions, and run on bloated web/Electron engines that consume **100MB - 300MB+ of RAM**, dropping your in-game FPS and adding input lag.

**Crosshair Free** is built in native C# / .NET 8 WPF with direct hardware-accelerated rendering and aggressive working-set memory purges. It uses **only 0.2 MB - 3 MB of RAM** (less RAM than Task Manager itself) while giving you pro-level customization and real-time weapon switching with fully customizable keybind triggers.

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 30 Competitive & Shotgun Reticles
* **Dedicated Shotgun Spreads**: Standard Bloom Circle, 4-Arm Bloom, Octagon Spread, Hexagon Spread, Shotgun Cross-Ring, Concentric Dual Rings, Heavy Diamond Bloom, 4-Corner Pellet Dots, Tri-Bloom.
* **Precision Rifle & Sniper Reticles**: Classic 4-Arm Cross, Center Dot Sniper, CS2 Static Precision, Overwatch Tri-Tick Circle, Target Bullseye, Double Tactical Chevron, Cross + Circle Combo, T-Style (No Top Arm), Tactical Chevron, Diamond, Box Square, Tri-Point Y-Cross, X-Cross (45°), Tactical Hollow Box Brackets, Apex Tri-Dot, CS/Valorant Classic Gap, Cyber Ring, and Sniper Mil-Dot hashes.
* **Visual Dropdown Icons**: Every reticle is shown with a live vector thumbnail before selection.

### 🔫 Dynamic Weapon Switching & Custom Keybinds
* Automatically swaps your on-screen reticle in real-time as you switch weapons in-game!
* **100% Configurable**: Bind any keys on your keyboard for both weapon profiles. Add new keys, remove keys, or customize to your exact game layout.
* **Interactive Key Recorder**: Simply click **+ Add Key** and press any key to register it instantly.
* **Smart Defaults Included**: Pre-configured with popular esports binds (e.g. `2`, `F`, `Shift`, `Q`, `Z`, `X`, `C` for AR and `3` for Shotgun).

### 🎨 2D Rainbow Color Picker & Esports Presets
* Custom saturation/brightness 2D spectrum canvas.
* 360° rainbow hue slider and direct HEX code input.
* 12 one-click pro esports color chips.

### ⚙️ Pixel-Perfect Crosshair Customization
* **Zero-Gap Sliders**: 1:1 smooth electric-blue controls for Arm Length, Radius, Line Thickness, Center Gap, Center Dot Size, and Outline Thickness.
* **Directional Nudging**: Offset your reticle pixel-by-pixel on the X and Y axes.
* **Background Contrast Preview**: Test against Dark, Sky Blue, Grass Green, and Snow White backdrops.

### 💾 Auto-Saving & Persistence
* All changes to sliders, colors, and custom keybinds save instantly to `settings.json` with an automatic AppData backup.

## 🎮 Keybind Controls

* **Assault Rifle Reticle**: Triggers on your configured AR keybinds (Default: `2`, `F`, `Shift`, `1`, `4`, `5`, `6`, `Q`, `Z`, `X`, `C`).
* **Shotgun Reticle**: Triggers on your configured Shotgun keybinds (Default: `3`, `NumPad 3`).
* **Overlay Toggle Key**: Default `F8` (toggles crosshair visibility on/off).

*(All keys can be completely changed, added, or deleted anytime in the **KEY SETTINGS** menu)*
